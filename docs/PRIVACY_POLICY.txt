1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Privacy Policy applies to the game "Flapcrypt" (also stylized as "FlapCrypt")
across all supported platforms and distribution channels, including:
• Mobile Applications (Google Play Store / Android)
• Web Editions (Hosted on Netlify / Modern Web Browsers)
• Desktop & Standalone Editions (Itch.io / Standalone Builds)
• Any related services operated by OLAI Digital Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

OLAI Digital Studios is committed to protecting the privacy of our players.
Flapcrypt is designed with an offline-first architecture to provide an enjoyable,
distraction-free, and privacy-respecting mechanical split-flap cryptogram puzzle
experience. We do not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable
information (PII).


2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flapcrypt does NOT require mandatory account creation, user registration, login,
or social media authentication. We do not collect, harvest, track, or store:
• Real names, physical home addresses, or telephone numbers
• Email addresses (unless you voluntarily contact our customer support team)
• Address books, contacts, camera, photos, or microphone data
• Precise geolocation or GPS coordinates
• Biometric or sensitive personal data
• Bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, or financial account credentials


3. LOCAL ON-DEVICE STORAGE (GAMEPLAY PROGRESS & SETTINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flapcrypt stores your puzzle progression and user preferences exclusively on your
local device using standard local storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage
and isolated application sandboxes).

This local data includes:
• Campaign progression, unlocked levels, solved cryptograms, and midway flap drum positions
  across all puzzle categories (including Quotes, Movie Lines, Proverbs, Riddles, Trivia, Wordplay,
  and future level expansions)
• Daily Challenge completion history, calendar records, and live streak counters
• In-game hint inventory balance (purchased or claimed free hint assists)
• Player preferences: Sound effects toggle, Haptic vibration feedback toggle, and
  Daily Reminder toggle & scheduled notification time
• In-app purchase entitlement status (e.g., "Remove Advertisements" active status)

This data remains strictly on your local device and is NEVER transmitted to external
cloud servers or third-party databases. You may reset or erase your data at any time
by selecting "Reset Progress" in the in-game Settings menu, using the "Reset All Flaps"
puzzle option, or by clearing the application's storage/cache in your Android or
browser device settings.


4. LOCAL DEVICE NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flapcrypt provides an optional "Daily Reminder" feature that alerts you to fresh Daily
Challenge split-flap puzzles.
• These notifications are scheduled and generated locally on your device by the
  operating system at a user-configured time (default: 10:00 AM).
• No external push notification servers, remote device tokens, or tracking systems
  are used.
• You can enable, disable, or adjust notification permissions at any time directly
  within the in-game Settings menu or through your device's System Notification settings.


5. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC MONETIZATION & IN-APP PURCHASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To support ongoing game development, level creation, and technical maintenance,
Flapcrypt utilizes platform-appropriate monetization models:

A. MOBILE APPLICATIONS (Google Play Store / Android):
• In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing):
  Players may purchase optional upgrades through the in-game Shop:
  - Remove Advertisements (Product ID: remove_ads): Permanently disables banner
    advertisements and interstitial breaks.
  - 25 Hints Pack (Product ID: hints_25): Consumable letter decryption assists.
  - 50 Hints Pack (Product ID: hints_50): Consumable letter decryption assists.
  All financial transactions, payments, and billing details are processed exclusively
  and securely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC). OLAI Digital Studios does not
  receive, process, handle, or store your credit card, debit card, bank account,
  or financial credentials.
  - Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/
  - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

• Advertising (Google AdMob):
  Where active, Flapcrypt may display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, or
  rewarded advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process pseudonymous
  device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse IP addresses,
  and diagnostic/performance data to deliver contextual ads and detect fraud.
  - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - AdMob Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID via your Android device
    settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete/Reset Advertising ID).

B. WEB EDITIONS (Netlify / Modern Web Browsers):
• Web Advertising:
  Web browser editions may display non-intrusive display advertisements. Third-party
  advertising partners may use cookies or browser storage to serve contextual ads.
  - Manage personalized Google web ads: https://adssettings.google.com
  - Opt out of third-party advertising cookies: https://www.aboutads.info/choices
    or https://optout.networkadvertising.org

C. DESKTOP & STANDALONE EDITIONS (Itch.io / Standalone Builds):
• 100% Offline & Ad-Free:
  The desktop standalone edition is completely self-contained, serves zero
  third-party advertisements, uses no tracking cookies, and initiates no marketing
  network requests during gameplay.

We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children
