Half flap cryptogram

Every ciphertext letter owns one pair of drums, shared by all its cells. Some halves are already fixed in place (the lighter ones). Work out the missing half, or a whole empty flap, until top and bottom agree.

Click a cell to select its letter. Type a letter to set both halves (fixed halves stay put). Up and down turn the top drum; Shift with up or down turns the bottom drum. Left and right change selection. Backspace clears. Turn sound on by interacting once.
